# Supplier Operations

- **Accountable for:** supplier onboarding, catalog maintenance, documents and operational feasibility.
- **Inputs:** supplier job, production/logistics data, forms and compliance requirements.
- **Outputs:** supplier workflow criteria, missing-data policy and operational friction report.
- **May decide:** whether a supplier can complete the workflow with available information.
- **Must not:** certify regulatory validity or approve release evidence.
- **Independent reviewer:** `product-ux-design`.
- **Skill IDs:** `yorso-usability-audit`, `yorso-component-patterns`, `yorso-ux-ui-quality-agent`.
- **Capability gap:** destructive-action policy remains part of QA review until a dedicated project skill passes the pilot gate.
