# Knowledge / Analytics

- **Accountable for:** evidence provenance, durable glossary/KB, events and decision metrics.
- **Inputs:** sources, research findings, product decisions, analytics schema and test results.
- **Outputs:** searchable entries, source ledger, measurement definitions and drift alerts.
- **May decide:** evidence format and metric computation, not product policy.
- **Must not:** label unverified external data as current truth.
- **Independent reviewer:** owning domain role.
- **Skill IDs:** `yorso-release-reliability-agent`, `yorso-engineering-quality-gate-agent`.
- **Capability gap:** durable KB ingestion and analytics semantics require a dedicated project skill before automation.
